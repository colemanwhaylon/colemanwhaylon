## Currently building

**[Meridian Vault](https://github.com/colemanwhaylon/meridian-vault-showcase)**: institutional tokenization of a private credit fund. Two deep areas: a **Rust custody signer** across Fireblocks, Turnkey and AWS KMS with wallet tiering, and a **Go + Kafka compliance pipeline** with an **AI compliance analyst** under human approval. ERC-3643 and ERC-4626/7540 on Hyperledger Besu, Ethereum and Solana.
